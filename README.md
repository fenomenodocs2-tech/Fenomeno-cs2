# 🎯 Fenômeno CS2

**Free Counter-Strike 2 career simulator in your browser.**

Play as a pro player or coach. Draft attributes from legends, climb from amateur Faceit to Majors, manage transfers, inventory, org trust, and leave a legacy.

🌐 **Play now:** [https://fenomenodocs2-tech.github.io/Fenomeno-cs2/](https://fenomenodocs2-tech.github.io/Fenomeno-cs2/)

---

## What is Fenômeno CS2?

Fenômeno CS2 is a **single-player CS2 career sim** inspired by classic football career games — adapted to the Counter-Strike scene.

You start young (amateur / Faceit / Games Club), draft attributes from real legends, join real teams, play real-style tournaments, and try to become **The Phenomenon**.

No download. No install. Works on **phone and PC**.

---

## Features

### 🎮 Career mode (Player)
- Attribute draft from CS legends (aim, clutch, gamesense…)
- Month-by-month progression
- Real teams & players (100+)
- Faceit / online cups → regional → S-tier → Majors
- Live Swiss bracket + playoffs
- Map veto, pre-match odds, post-match interviews
- Ranking (world + Faceit-style elo)
- Salaries, sponsors, stickers, agent transfers
- Training, bootcamp, energy, form & chemistry
- Inventory: skins, knives, gloves, stickers, music kits (equip for boosts)
- Social media, fans, weekly challenges
- HLTV-style profile, awards & career legacy

### 🎧 Coach mode (Beta)
- Manage the bench instead of the server
- Live calls: timeout, anti-eco, force, anti-strat, defaults, stand-in
- Org pressure & monthly org goals
- Roster tools, line rotation, trust
- Titles count for the coach — **MVP is always a player**

### 📅 Systems
- Weekly challenges (home screen)
- Concrete contract offers (salary, years, buyout)
- Org goals every month (trust up/down)
- Offline-friendly (PWA / local save)
- Multi-language (PT / EN / ES and more — some in beta)
- Patch notes & feedback

---

## How to play

1. Open the link above  
2. Create your career (Player or Coach)  
3. Draft attributes  
4. Pick a starting team (by nationality / level)  
5. Train, play tournaments, manage money & transfers  
6. Climb ranking → Majors → HLTV #1 → legacy  

**Tip:** Don’t skip training for too long — form and level drop over time.
